# Unit 56: Console scaffold

## Goal

The Next.js 15 App Router project under `console/` with TypeScript strict;
the vendored `industry.css` and the console-owned `console-tokens.css`
(dark theme, `--sem-*`, `--font-mono`); `Shell`, `ThemeRoot`,
`KeyboardMap`, `Freshness`; the `/unsupported` route and the under-1280 px
redirect; `lib/api.ts` with the generated `api.types.ts`; the
`/healthz`-backed status in the header; the six routes rendering their
titles with placeholder data in light and dark (PRD §Capability 6; TRD
§5.1, §5.2; ui-context.md; code-standards · TypeScript, Next.js, Styling).

## Design

Terminal only. `console/` is created with `create-next-app` (App Router,
TypeScript, ESLint, no Tailwind, `src/` off) and trimmed: two global
stylesheets in the root layout in order (`styles/industry.css` copied from
the design package's `styles.css`, never edited; `styles/console-tokens.css`
with the dark values, the five semantic roles, the mono face and the
reduced-motion rule), Google Fonts for Barlow, Barlow Condensed and
JetBrains Mono, `tsconfig` with `strict`, `noUncheckedIndexedAccess` and
`exactOptionalPropertyTypes`. `lib/api.ts` is the one orchestrator client:
server-side `fetch` to `ORCHESTRATOR_URL` with the service token and
`X-Factory-Actor` headers, `cache: 'no-store'` for live reads and tags for
config and eval reads, the standard error body surfaced as `ApiError`;
`lib/api.types.ts` is generated by `openapi-typescript` from the committed
`openapi.json` (`npm run api:types`, checked by a test that regenerates and
diffs). `Shell` renders the header (installation name from
`/api/v1/installation`, the six-screen nav with the Approvals badge from
`/needs-human/count`, the `FACTORY_ENABLED` pill from `/healthz`, a login
pill placeholder until unit 57), the screen title and a `Freshness` stamp;
`ThemeRoot` applies `prefers-color-scheme` with the best-effort
`localStorage` override on `data-theme`; `KeyboardMap` binds `1`–`6` to the
screens and `?` to the map dialog; `ViewportGuard` in the root layout
redirects under 1280 px to `/unsupported` ("Factory Console needs a window
at least 1280 px wide"). The six routes exist with their titles and
placeholder tables; the authz `console/lib/authz.ts` from unit 54 gets its
vitest run here.
